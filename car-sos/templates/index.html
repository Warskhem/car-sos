How It Works

1

Each car gets a unique QR code sticker placed on the windshield or dashboard.

2

The owner scans the QR to register vehicle details and 3 emergency contacts.

3

In an accident, a rescuer scans the QR with their phone — no app required.

4

SOS alert with GPS location and beep is sent instantly to all emergency contacts.

Privacy First

🔒

Your data stays private

Emergency contacts are never revealed to the person scanning the QR code. Only vehicle information is shown on the SOS page.
